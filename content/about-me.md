I'm Vidol Chalamov, a naturally curious individual with an insatiable appetite for technology. Born on 29th February 1996 in Plovdiv, Bulgaria, I've always been driven by a deep-seated passion for understanding and harnessing the power of innovation.

## Professional Background

My academic journey took me through the Profiled School of Electrical Engineering and Electronics in Plovdiv, Bulgaria, and the University of Plovdiv, where I cultivated a profound fascination for programming and all things tech. This curiosity has been the driving force behind my career, guiding me through various roles in the tech industry.

At **Primeholding JSC**, I've evolved from an **Intern .NET Developer** to a **Senior Software Engineer**. Throughout this journey, my strong technical knowledge has been my compass. I've delved into diverse technologies, from backend development with GoLang, .NET, NodeJS, and gRPC, to skillfully managing databases and crafting responsive user interfaces with Typescript, React, and Angular.
